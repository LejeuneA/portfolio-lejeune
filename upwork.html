The advantage of design and code together

Many small digital projects lose quality between design and implementation. Spacing changes, responsive behaviour breaks, components become inconsistent and the final interface no longer feels like the original concept.

My strength is working across that gap. I bring experienced visual and UI design judgement together with practical frontend awareness, so the interface can move from Figma to working UI with more clarity, consistency and attention to detail.
